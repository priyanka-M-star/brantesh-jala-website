Our mission

To make hard water easier to live with, through quality products and dependable service.

Easier everyday water

Conditioners for homes, apartments and businesses that help reduce scale in pipes, taps and heaters, with support close by in Davanagere.

Support for farms and local business

Solutions for drip lines, dairies, poultry sheds and pools, so lime scale causes fewer blockages and less upkeep.

Water-wise by design

Salt-free units, so there is no salt to buy and no brine sent down the drain. Quality and service is our motto.

Our solutions
